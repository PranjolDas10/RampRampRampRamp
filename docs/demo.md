# 90-second demo

**Setup:** `npm run dev`. Open `/personal` and `/ledgerline` in one window, with `localhost:3000` (dashboard) beside it if you have a second screen. Click ↻ on the Ledgerline presenter bar to reset. Keep the demo tab in front: background tabs slow the cursor down.

**One-time, for "Run on this Mac":** on `/personal`, click *First time on this Mac? Set up* → **Copy script** → **Open Shortcuts** → add a *Run Shell Script* action, paste, and name it **Start my workday**. Turn on *Allow Running Scripts* (Shortcuts → Settings → Advanced). Click Run once and allow the "Open Shortcuts?" prompt.

## 0:00–0:25 · Personal: my routine becomes the team's (`/personal`)

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Point at the strip (9 of 10 days) | "cl1ck saw my morning routine on 9 of the last 10 days and saved it as a workflow." |

## 0:25–1:05 · Business: one person's habit, automated for the whole team (`/ledgerline`)

Press **Play** on the presenter bar for each step. The cursor's label shows who is acting (*Dana · by hand* or *cl1ck*).
